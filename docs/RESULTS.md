# Results

**Every result here is in simulation (MuJoCo, SO-101, cube into a tray).** Nothing has been run on a real robot.

The demos come from a **scripted expert** that follows the cube path extracted from one stock video. They are not retargeted human motion.

Sources, all in this repository:
- `REPORT.md` and `gates/*.md` on branch `report/overnight`: the final run report and the pass/fail gates.
- The `README_*.md` files on the experiment branches, which quote `SCOREBOARD.md` and gate files. `SCOREBOARD.md` is generated by `eval/scoreboard.py` (branch `small/smolvla-act`) and is not committed.

Numbers are copied as they appear there. "k/n" counts episodes; ranges in brackets are 95% Wilson intervals.

## 1. Definitions

**Task.** Pick a 20–26 mm cube and place it in an 11 × 11 cm tray. Instruction string: "pick up the cube and place it in the bin".

**Phase-free verifier** (`datagen/r2s/verify_policy.py`, branch `eval/harness`). It judges a policy from physics alone, without the expert's phase labels.
- **Carry:** both jaws touch the cube, the cube is more than 5 mm above its settled height, and it touches neither the table nor the bin floor.
- **Release:** the carry ends while the jaw is opening. Any other end of a carry counts as a drop or slip.
- **L_task** (task done): the cube was carried with both jaws, a carry ended over the bin, the cube ends in the bin region resting, and no jaw touches it in the last 0.5 s.
- **L_full** adds:
  - Physical-honesty clauses: drop or slip; release height at most 10 mm; cube speed at most 0.5 m/s; no foreign contact while carried; no arm-body contact with the cube; no arm-table, arm-bin or self collision; joint limits; torque saturation; target speed cap; robot contact penetration deeper than 2 mm for 3 or more steps; a lift of at least 2 cm.
  - Style clauses, also reported separately: cube disturbed more than 10 mm before the grasp; tracking error.

**Seeds.** Training data used seed 1. Held-out in-range scenes: seed 3000. Out-of-range ladder: seed 4000, ids 0–49. Smoke tests: seed 1000. No eval seed was used in training.

**Statistics** (`eval/scoreboard.py`).
- Wilson 95% interval on every rate.
- Exact McNemar test (two-sided binomial test) on the episodes two runs share, joined on (seed, attempt).
- Holm correction across all 204 tests in the scoreboard.
- A difference is claimed only if Holm p < 0.05; otherwise the verdict is "not distinguishable at n=…".
- Policy sampling noise is not seeded per episode, so policy evals are not bit-reproducible. The scenes are.

## 7. Out-of-range ladder

Each rung changes one factor outside the training range, on the same 50 scenes (seed 4000, ids 0–49). Everything else stays identical to the `none` run. Full rung definitions: `docs/OOD_LADDER.md` on branch `eval/ood-ladder`.

- `camera`: front camera height ±12–16 cm and pitch ±12–15°, FOV 51°. Single-axis sub-rungs: `camera_height`, `camera_pitch`, `camera_yaw` (±10–15°), `camera_distance` (±8–12 cm).
- `exposure`: brightness ×0.5/×1.6, contrast ×0.6/×1.4, or white-balance gain 0.8/1.2.
- `colour`: magenta cube, near-black bin, saturated green table, lights dimmer or brighter than any training scene.
- `combo`: the camera draws plus the colour draws.
- `distractor`: 1–2 extra cylinders or spheres (20–35 mm); putting one in the bin fails the episode.

**Expert ceiling** on every rung: 17/20 L_full, 19/20 L_task. The expert plans from state, so images do not affect it.

**MolmoAct2, checkpoint 023400, n = 50 per rung:**

## 8. Data engine: narrow vs wide ACT

Same ACT recipe, same scenes, paired. The wide dataset widens, per episode, the front camera (height ±15 cm, pitch ±15°, yaw ±15°, distance ±10 cm, FOV 45 ± 8°) and image exposure on both cameras. Scenes, physics and trajectories are identical to the narrow dataset.

| Test set | n | L_full narrow → wide | L_task narrow → wide |
|---|---|---|---|
| in-range s3000 | 100 | 17 → 29 | 69 → 76 |
| s4000 none | 50 | 7 → 16 | 31 → 39 |
| camera | 50 | 10 → 21 | 30 → 44 * |
| exposure | 50 | 10 → 17 | 30 → 43 |
| colour | 50 | **2 → 18 \*\*** | 18 → 34 * |
| combo | 50 | 3 → 13 | 19 → 33 |
| distractor | 50 | 1 → 7 | 12 → 17 |
| camera_pitch | 50 | 5 → 19 * | 25 → 36 |
| camera_distance | 50 | **4 → 20 \*\*** | 25 → 37 |

\*\* "B better (significant)" after Holm over the whole scoreboard (Holm p 0.027 each); this is the official verdict. \* significant only within the pre-specified 22-test narrow-vs-wide family. Unmarked: "not distinguishable at n=50".

Read before quoting:
- Wide ≥ narrow on all 22 paired comparisons (raw), but only the starred ones are significant.
- Most camera rungs lie inside the wide model's training ranges. Gains there show coverage, not out-of-range generalisation.
- Colours were not widened, so the colour gain (2 → 18) is the cleaner evidence that breadth helps.
- Wide data did not fix distractors: ACT-wide still drops 39 to 17 of 50 on L_task, "significant drop" (Holm p 9.3e-05).

## 9. Memory under an emulated laptop cap

`env/capped.py` caps PyTorch's allocator to emulate a smaller card on the MI300X. Wording from the gate: "fits under an emulated 5.0 GiB PyTorch allocator cap (proxy for a 6 GB laptop card; context and driver overhead on CUDA not measured)"; the same applies to 7.0 GiB (proxy for 8 GB).

| Model | Largest batch that fits | Reserved |
|---|---|---|
| SmolVLA | batch 16 under 7.0 GiB | 6.11 GB |
| SmolVLA | batch 8 under 5.0 GiB | 3.80 GB |
| ACT | batch 32 under 5.0 GiB | 4.13 GB |

The next batch size up runs out of memory. Inference: ACT 0.32 GB, SmolVLA 0.99 GB. Capped and uncapped loss curves match step by step. All timings are MI300X timings, not laptop timings; nothing was run on a laptop GPU.

## 10. Negative and inconclusive results

### Named cube among three (language)
Three cubes on the table; the instruction names one colour. Training targets are red, green and blue; yellow is never a target. Verdict: **FAIL.** Neither model learned the task at 600 demos. The named cube moved in 13–15% of held-out scenes, and no cube moved in about 75% of runs. The named-cube rates are "not distinguishable at n=60". "Language beats no-language" is not shown.

| Test | SmolVLA-multi (reads language) | ACT-multi (no language) |
|---|---|---|
| Held-out ids 0–59, training templates: named cube moved | 9/60 (15%, 8.1–26.1%) | 8/60 (13%, 6.9–24.2%) |
| Same: named cube in tray | 5/60 | 5/60 |
| Held-out paraphrases, ids 0–29: named | 2/30 (7%, 1.8–21.3%) | not run |
| Swap, ids 0–19 × 3 colours: named | 5/60 | 4/60 |
| Yellow target, ids 100–119 (never a training target) | 1/20 | 3/20 |

### V-JEPA 2 success judge
Pre-registered gate: the V-JEPA 2 probe must beat the best last-frame baseline by at least 0.02 AUC with paired DeLong p < 0.05. Verdict: **FAIL (JEPA lost).**

| Contender, label y_full | test (164 pos / 71 neg) | cam_test (96 / 50) |
|---|---|---|
| Probe, mean-pooled V-JEPA 2, front camera, 16 frames (gated) | 0.784 [0.720, 0.849] | 0.829 [0.756, 0.901] |
| Last frame, V-JEPA | 0.799 [0.736, 0.861] | 0.752 [0.666, 0.838] |
| Last frame, DINOv2 | 0.802 [0.737, 0.868] | 0.822 [0.752, 0.893] |
| Shuffled labels (20×) | mean 0.512 | mean 0.496 |

- Gate: probe − last-frame DINOv2 = −0.018, CI [−0.084, +0.048], p = 0.59.
- Not pre-registered, so treat as hypotheses: the 8 time slots kept in order scored 0.866, and wrist-camera video 0.912 against 0.798 for the wrist last frame.

### Sim-to-real visual gap
Fréchet distance in V-JEPA 2 features, 200 clips against 200, over 20 random half-splits:

| Comparison | Full 1024-d |
|---|---|
| real vs real (floor) | 15.0 (11.4–18.6) |
| sim vs sim (floor) | 13.2 (11.9–14.7) |
| sim vs real | 1328.8 (1328.6–1329.3) |

The sim-vs-real gap is about 89 times the real floor, and a classifier separates sim from real perfectly (5-fold AUC 1.000). Moving the camera within ±8 cm does not change it. The mismatch is viewpoint, scene content and the flat renders.

### Real-camera overlay of the arm
Real arm mask against the simulated arm, rendered at the real joint readings, on 10 held-out frames. Verdict: **INCONCLUSIVE.** Held-out mean IoU 0.49 (0.47 on a repeat) against a 0.8 pass mark. Even a separate fitted camera per frame only reaches 0.66, and the test cannot tell the default joint mapping from a 20° error. A flipped shoulder sign is detected (0.37).

### Not run or dropped
- **π0.5:** blocked. The `google/paligemma-3b-pt-224` tokenizer is gated and access was not granted.
- **Filtered-BC "practice round":** the pipeline and its controls were built and CPU-tested end to end, then dropped for time.
- **Parallel policy replicas:** multi-replica inference hung on this machine (pools of 8 and 4, and 4 independent processes). One process at a time is reliable, so all evals ran that way.
- **Drawer, multi-stage and dexterous tasks:** next steps only.
